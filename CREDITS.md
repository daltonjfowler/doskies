# Credits

## Weather data

Weather data by [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/), licensed under CC BY 4.0. Free and keyless;
no account or API key is used or required. DOSkies never sends personal data to Open-Meteo, only
the coordinates needed for a forecast.

## Fonts and glyphs

The DOS/pixel bitmap font and the ten hand-drawn weather glyphs are added by hand in the T5 art
pass (see PLAN.md); this section will record their license and attribution once bundled. Any
bundled font or glyph asset must be openly licensed (SIL OFL, or CC BY / CC BY-SA with
attribution). No "free for personal use only" fonts.
